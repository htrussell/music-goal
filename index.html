                    SiriusXM Pandora • Strategic AI Feature Spec
                

                
Goal-Based Audio Listening System

                

                    A machine-learning and LLM-assisted listening mode enabling listeners to establish explicit, time-bounded listening goals with strict topic exclusions that immediately override historical collaborative filtering habits.
                

            

        


                
        

            

                

                     Problem Statement
                

                

                    Adult SiriusXM Pandora Premium listeners who want to change their listening habits frequently receive familiar recommendations that conflict with their current intentions—such as serialized fiction podcasts instead of economics or sports.
                

                

                    Under traditional collaborative filtering, listeners must search manually, skip repetitive recommendations, or continually train algorithms with thumbs-down signals, consuming limited listening time and diminishing subscription value.
                

            


            

                

                     How SiriusXM Can Implement It
                

                

                    SiriusXM's core differentiation is its proprietary Music Genome Project (450+ musical attributes) and exclusive talk/sports programming. The Build/Buy/Partner analysis recommends a Partner model with a System Integrator (Slalom / EPAM) for a 16-week sprint.
                

                

                    The SI deploys the LLM orchestration gateway, AI guardrails, and knapsack time-budget packing inside SiriusXM’s AWS VPC with zero-data-retention, safeguarding user privacy (CCPA/CPRA, VPPA) while transferring full IP ownership.
                

            

        


        
        

            

                

                    
                

                
Natural Language Intent

                
Converts freeform input (up to 500 chars) into normalized goals, preferred topics, and hard negative exclusions without retraining existing recommender models.

            


            

                

                    
                

                
Exact Duration Budgeting

                
Enforces 1–120 minute queue constraints, packing audio candidates precisely to prevent over-run and respect limited user listening intervals.

            


            

                

                    
                

                
Deterministic Exclusions

                
Guarantees zero unwanted content slips into the queue. Excluded categories or tags are filtered with code-level guards before playback.

            


            

                

                    
                

                
Habit Override & Fallback

                
Overrides habitual listening (e.g., fiction podcasts) and dynamically identifies popular tracks fitting preferred topics if the immediate pool yields no match.
